Things people ask first

I'm embarrassed by how bad things are. Should I clean up first?

No. Cleaning up is the job. The sooner we start, the less it costs to fix.

How is this different from a bookkeeper or a CPA?

A bookkeeper records transactions. A CPA usually prepares taxes and statements. I make sense of the numbers, plan cash, deal with problems and help you decide what to do. I work with both.

Do I have to replace my bookkeeper or accountant?

Usually not. If they're doing good work, we keep them. If something isn't working, I'll tell you plainly.

What size businesses do you work with?

Mostly $500,000 to $25 million in annual revenue: complicated enough to need real financial help, often too small for a full-time CFO.

Do you work outside the United States?

Yes. I work across the U.S. and have spent years with companies in Europe, especially Poland.

Are you a CPA or tax attorney?

No. For filings and legal questions I work with yours, or introduce you to people I trust.
